TALKCARE SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you find the right next step, at your own pace.

What is TalkCare?

TalkCare is an informational mental health and justice-support platform that brings together accessible resources, support pathways, and ways to raise a concern.

How does TalkCare support mental health and justice needs?

It connects people with emotional-care resources, practical guidance, information about rights and fairness, and pathways for seeking respectful support.

Is my information private?

Please share only what feels appropriate. This prototype does not provide a secure clinical or legal record, so avoid entering sensitive information.

How do I report a concern?

Choose a pathway or open Reports from the dashboard, then complete the concern form with the details you feel comfortable sharing.

How can I book a session?

Signed-in users can choose Book a Session from the dashboard to explore the available request flow. Availability and confirmation are not connected in this prototype.

What are JusticeCare and ThoMatt?

They are TalkCare pathway concepts: JusticeCare focuses on rights, fairness, and concerns, while ThoMatt focuses on mental health consultation and support.

Can I save articles or resources?

Yes. Use the heart on an article to add or remove it from your in-session favorites. Favorites are cleared when the page session ends.

What should I do in an emergency?

TalkCare is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted crisis professional immediately.
