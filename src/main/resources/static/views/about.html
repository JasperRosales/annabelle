The Mental Health and Justice web application aims to create a safe, accessible, and supportive environment where individuals can openly express their emotions, concerns, and experiences without fear of judgment or discrimination.

It provides users with access to appropriate mental health resources, guidance, and information about their rights. By connecting mental well-being with fairness and justice, the application empowers clients to seek help, understand their rights, feel heard, and receive respectful and appropriate support.

About

JusticeCare is a support network for those who have faced extreme situations such as partner abuse, sexual assault and other civil and domestic injustices. Often speaking out about these scenarios can be scary for victims. However, with this network we offer a supportive, non-judgmental and completely confidential place for survivors to speak about their experience, offering immediate emotional/social support and resources to help them.

Once you're safe and settled, we can help connect you with legal professionals, police and statutory bodies to ensure real justice is delivered. JusticeCare helps you to report any issues you may have faced, whether it be a personal grievance, act of violence or civil injustice. We can provide you with protection orders, legal advice and ongoing support with finding somewhere safe. There is no need for any victim to suffer in silence as JusticeCare can turn your reported violation into real legal protection and help you on your road to recovery.
