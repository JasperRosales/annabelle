JUSTICE · FOR MY RIGHTS

Understand your options.

You deserve to be treated with dignity. Start by identifying what happened, what outcome you hope for, and which trusted local service can explain your rights.

A practical first step

Write down the key facts for yourself, including dates and any questions you want answered. If you need legal guidance or wish to raise a concern, contact a qualified local legal-aid or advocacy service. You can choose how much to share.

INDIVIDUAL · FOR MYSELF

Make room for yourself.

You do not have to have everything figured out. A small pause can help you notice what you need and decide on one manageable next step.

Try a gentle grounding moment

Feel your feet on the ground. Notice three things you can see, two things you can hear, and one thing you can feel. Breathe at a comfortable pace. If you would like more support, consider reaching out to a trusted person or qualified mental health professional.

PEERS · FOR OTHERS

Be there, without carrying it alone.

Showing up for someone can start with listening. Respect their choices, avoid pressure, and remember that your own well-being matters too.

A caring way to begin

YOU ARE NOT ALONE.

TalkCare is designed to provide a secure, accessible, and confidential platform that connects individuals with mental health support and reliable resources. Its purpose is to encourage people to openly address their mental health concerns, reduce the barriers associated with seeking help, and promote awareness of their rights. Through accessible support and information, TalkCare aims to contribute to a more compassionate, informed, and equitable community.

About

JusticeCare is a support network for those who have faced extreme situations such as partner abuse, sexual assault and other civil and domestic injustices. Often speaking out about these scenarios can be scary for victims. However, with this network we offer a supportive, non-judgmental and completely confidential place for survivors to speak about their experience, offering immediate emotional/social support and resources to help them.

Once you're safe and settled, we can help connect you with legal professionals, police and statutory bodies to ensure real justice is delivered. JusticeCare helps you to report any issues you may have faced, whether it be a personal grievance, act of violence or civil injustice. We can provide you with protection orders, legal advice and ongoing support with finding somewhere safe. There is no need for any victim to suffer in silence as JusticeCare can turn your reported violation into real legal protection and help you on your road to recovery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you find the right next step, at your own pace.

What is TalkCare?

TalkCare is an informational mental health and justice-support platform that brings together accessible resources, support pathways, and ways to raise a concern.

How does TalkCare support mental health and justice needs?

It connects people with emotional-care resources, practical guidance, information about rights and fairness, and pathways for seeking respectful support.

Is my information private?

Please share only what feels appropriate. This prototype does not provide a secure clinical or legal record, so avoid entering sensitive information.

How do I report a concern?

Choose a pathway or open Reports from the dashboard, then complete the concern form with the details you feel comfortable sharing.

How can I book a session?

Signed-in users can choose Book a Session from the dashboard to explore the available request flow. Availability and confirmation are not connected in this prototype.

What are JusticeCare and ThoMatt?

They are TalkCare pathway concepts: JusticeCare focuses on rights, fairness, and concerns, while ThoMatt focuses on mental health consultation and support.

Can I save articles or resources?

Yes. Use the heart on an article to add or remove it from your in-session favorites. Favorites are cleared when the page session ends.

What should I do in an emergency?

TalkCare is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted crisis professional immediately.
